About DALI Control Platform

A clear engineering experience connecting DALI controllers, commissioning and field maintenance for professional lighting projects.

01

Product position

The current engineering software brings together controller discovery and management, DALI addressing, groups and scenes, device readings, scoped parameter settings, and capability-gated dimming and DT8 colour controls. Project files, batch jobs, reports and AI remain future work.

02

Who it serves

The product is designed for system integrators, commissioning engineers, lighting OEMs, delivery teams, channel partners and organisations that maintain professional lighting systems.

03

Business value

Consistent objects, states and workflows reduce tool switching and make on-site configuration clearer, while preserving room for maintenance, integration and brand partnerships.

04

Deployment and platforms

The current direction covers direct Type-C and LAN access, with screen formats planned for Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android phones and Android tablets. Final platforms, features and delivery methods are subject to released product and contract materials.

05

Standards and capability boundaries

Capabilities are planned around IEC 62386, DALI-2, DT6, DT8 and D4i industry frameworks. “Standards-led” does not mean third-party certification; certifications, compatibility lists and performance claims will be used only after verification and formal release.

06

Intended-use boundary

This product is intended for professional lighting engineering. It must not be the sole control for life safety, emergency egress or other safety-critical systems without dedicated assessment, redundancy and regulatory confirmation.

07

AI direction

Future AI capabilities may explore natural-language intent, scene recommendations, anomaly signals and guided commissioning. AI descriptions on this site are roadmap items, not currently delivered features.

08

Ways to work together

Potential collaboration spans hardware formats, software branding, platform adaptation, system integration, channel delivery and long-term operations. Commercial scope, service levels, intellectual property and data responsibilities must be defined by contract.
