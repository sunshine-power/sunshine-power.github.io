IN-APP HELP

Status indicators

The dots in the project tree describe the current state of each node. They are not hierarchy markers.

Read the tree from summary to detail

A parent dot summarizes the visible business branch; a child dot describes its own connection or scan result.

Status colors

Colors follow the active application theme. The meaning stays the same in light and dark themes.

A note about repeated green dots

The connected-controller group and its controller children can both be green. The group means that at least one controller is online; the child means that this specific controller has a live session.
