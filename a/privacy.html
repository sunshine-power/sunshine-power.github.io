Privacy Notice

This page is a product-level notice aligned with the current product scope. Before public release, the actual operator must add its legal name, contact address, privacy email, regional terms and effective date, followed by legal review.

Scope

This notice describes current software workflows and planned desktop and mobile engineering use with companion DALI controllers or gateways. Platform availability and feature scope depend on the released version. Customer-built platforms, third-party operating systems and external services follow their own terms.

Our principles

  • Limit processing to the information needed to complete professional lighting tasks.
  • Prefer processing on the user device and within controlled local networks.
  • Explain purpose, scope and controls before introducing cloud, remote or AI capabilities.

Information we may process

Project and configuration data

Controller and line identifiers, short addresses, groups, scenes and parameters. Project-file export and snapshots are still in development.

Device and connection data

Local network addresses, connection state, software and firmware versions, device capabilities, operation times and update results.

Diagnostic information

Operation events, communication status, errors and fault context used for on-site assessment and troubleshooting.

Support material

Contact details, project extracts, logs or attachments are processed only when you actively request support.

The product is not designed to collect occupant identity, contacts, advertising identifiers, payment data or unrelated precise location. Project names, network addresses and logs may still qualify as personal data under some laws and should be managed under your organisation’s access rules.

Why information is processed

Information is used to discover and connect controllers, perform available configuration and control tasks, display status, diagnose faults and provide requested support. Future project-file and backup functions will be documented when released. It is not used for selling personal information or advertising profiles.

Local processing and storage

Current workflows centre on Type-C, controlled local networks and on-device diagnostics. Project-file export and backup are still in development. This website stores only language and theme preferences in your browser and currently uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

System permissions

Depending on the platform and feature, the software may request local network access, companion device connections or update package selection. File permissions will apply when related functions are released. Permissions can be managed in operating system settings; denying access may prevent the related function from working.

Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell engineering data or personal information. Minimum necessary information may be provided only for support you authorise, contracted service provision, a corporate transaction or a legal requirement. If future cloud services cause international transfers, the receiving region, safeguards and available choices will be explained before activation.

Future AI capabilities

AI content on this site describes a roadmap and does not mean project data is currently uploaded. If cloud analysis, natural-language control or model services are introduced, we will separately explain the data used, processing location, retention, human review and opt-out controls.

Your controls and rights

You can manage available local data and permissions through the software and operating system. Project-file export and deletion controls are not yet complete. Applicable access, deletion or restriction requests will use the official privacy channel once the operator details are published.

Security and responsibility

Controlled connections and data minimisation can reduce risk. Customers remain responsible for protecting networks, device credentials and any exported records, and for limiting unauthorised access to control systems. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This product is intended for professional lighting projects and organisational users. It does not provide a consumer service to children or intentionally seek to collect children’s personal information.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when there are material changes to data types, purposes, cloud capabilities, AI features or applicable law, with appropriate notice through the software, release page or agreed contractual channel.

Contact and release details

The operator name, privacy contact, email address, postal address and regional rights information will be configured before product release. The current showcase must not use a sample email address for genuine privacy requests.
