Preview data notice

The formal privacy policy will accompany the full software release

This page describes how the current product handles data. The operator, privacy contact and regional terms will be published with the full software release. For product inquiries, use the official Suretron website.

Scope

This notice covers connections, configuration, diagnostics and support between the current desktop engineering software and companion DALI controllers or gateways. Data practices for future platforms will be explained when those versions are released. Third-party systems and services follow their own terms.

Our principles

  • Limit processing to the information needed to complete professional lighting tasks.
  • Prefer processing engineering data on the user device and within controlled local networks.
  • Explain purpose, scope and controls before introducing cloud, remote or AI capabilities.

Information we may process

Project and configuration data

Engineering configuration data such as controller and line identifiers, short addresses, groups, scenes and parameters. Project-file export and snapshots are not available in the current version.

Device and connection data

Local network addresses, connection state, software and firmware versions, device capabilities and relevant operation records.

Diagnostic information

Operation events, communication status, errors and fault context used for on-site assessment and troubleshooting.

Support material

Contact details, project extracts, logs or attachments are processed only when you actively request support.

The product is not designed to collect occupant identity, contacts, advertising identifiers, payment data or unrelated precise location. Project names, network addresses and logs may still qualify as personal data under some laws and should be managed under your organisation’s access rules.

Why information is processed

Information is used to discover and connect controllers, perform currently available configuration and control, show status, diagnose issues and provide support you request. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising profiles.

Local processing and storage

Current workflows use Type-C, controlled local networks and on-device diagnostics without requiring a cloud account. This website stores only language and theme preferences in your browser and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

System permissions

Depending on the feature in use, the software may request local network or companion-device access. You can manage permissions in your operating system; denying them may prevent related functions from working. File permissions will be explained if file features are released.

Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell engineering data or personal information. Minimum necessary information may be provided only for support you authorise, contracted service provision, a corporate transaction or a legal requirement. If future cloud services cause international transfers, the receiving region, safeguards and available choices will be explained before activation.

Future AI capabilities

AI assistance is not yet available, and the AI information on this site does not mean engineering data is currently uploaded. If cloud analysis or model services are introduced, we will explain the data scope, processing location, retention and available choices before activation.

Your controls and rights

You can manage currently available local data and permissions through the software and operating system. Project-file export and related deletion controls are not yet available. For personal-data questions or applicable rights requests, use the contact details on the official Suretron website.

Security and responsibility

Local processing and controlled networks help limit data exposure. Protect devices, networks and system access, and share diagnostic material carefully. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This product is intended for professional lighting projects and organisational users. It does not provide a consumer service to children or intentionally seek to collect children’s personal information.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when there are material changes to data types, purposes, cloud capabilities, AI features or applicable law, with appropriate notice through the software, release page or agreed contractual channel.
